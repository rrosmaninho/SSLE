==> Post-mortem
Kill chain: deliver (FIM) -> execute -> C2 channel (netwatch) -> isolate.
Three independent detections: the dropped binary (FIM), the outbound
connection to :4444 (netwatch), and - had the attacker persisted - a new
cron/systemd unit (rootcheck).
Response isolates rather than kills: the service is cut out of the
architecture (deregistered, C2 blocked) but the container is left intact
for forensics. Detection latency is bounded by the 30s netwatch poll,
which is the window an attacker has before isolation - a real limitation.
Containment held throughout: lab-lockdown kept Metasploit traffic off the
internet, and nftables kept the lab off the home LAN.
