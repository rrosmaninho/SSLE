==> PHASE 6 - Post-mortem
Interpretation:
 - Compare p95 and throughput: baseline vs constrained vs paired attack.
 - Throughput alone does not prove loss of availability; the evidence is
   the combination of raised p95, saturated target CPU, and any non-2xx /
   failed requests appearing only under the paired load.
 - Detection correlates two independent signals: Prometheus p95 (server
   latency) and the Wazuh frequency rule (request volume per source).
 - Response: firewall-drop on the sustained-flood rule (100102), wired to
   the severe case so a brief legitimate spike does not auto-block a client.
 - Snapshot ingestion/pre-ddos allows a clean revert.
